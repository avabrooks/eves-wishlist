The Add button

Tap it while you're looking at something in any shop. The name, price and photo go straight into your wishlist.

1. Get the button

Set this up once per device. It takes about a minute.

Add to Eve's Wishlist

Copied. Now follow the steps for your device below.

Show the code

On a computer

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Ctrl+Shift+B (Mac: Cmd+Shift+B).
  2. Drag the pink Add to Eve's Wishlist button above up onto the bookmarks bar.
  3. On any product page, click it. Your wishlist opens with the details filled in. Check them, pick a size, and save.

On iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap Copy button code above.
  2. In Safari, open any page. Tap the Share button, then Add Bookmark, name it Add to Wishlist, and save.
  3. Tap the book icon, open your bookmarks, and tap Edit. Tap the Add to Wishlist bookmark, tap its web address, clear it, paste the copied code, then tap Done.
  4. On a product page: tap the book icon, then tap Add to Wishlist. Your wishlist opens with the details filled in.

This needs Safari. Buttons like this don't work inside the shop's own app, but in the app you can tap Share, copy the link, and paste it into the wishlist.

Want one tap from any app on your phone? Set up the Share menu shortcut.

Rarely, a shop blocks buttons like this. Then paste the link into the wishlist and fill in whatever is missing. Back to the wishlist
